Questions

Can I download it yet?

Not yet. ChiroBoards is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Follow @getchiroboards to hear when it's live.

Is ChiroBoards connected to the NBCE?

No. ChiroBoards is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NBCE. Always check the current test plan on the NBCE's own website.

Do I need an account?

No. You can sign in with Apple in one tap if you like, but it's optional, there's no password to make and we don't ask for your email. Your progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

How does the app know my level?

When you start, a short 20-question level check covers all six subjects in the same proportions as the exam. It shows your strongest and weakest subject and points your first sessions where you need them. You can skip it and take it later.

Does my progress move between my devices?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs by itself across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. There's nothing to set up.

How do I get help?

Email chiroboards0@gmail.com or see the Support page.
