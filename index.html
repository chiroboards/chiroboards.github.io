Why C is correct

Diphtheria toxin ADP-ribosylates EF-2, halting protein synthesis in host cells.

Why the others are wrong

A. SNARE cleavage is the mechanism of botulinum and tetanus toxins.

B. This describes cholera and heat-labile E. coli toxin.

D. This describes Shiga toxin.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I download it yet?

Not yet. ChiroBoards is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Follow @getchiroboards on Instagram to hear when it's live.

Is ChiroBoards connected to the NBCE?

No. ChiroBoards is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NBCE. All questions are original practice items, not real exam questions. Always check the current test plan on the NBCE's own website.

Do I need an account?

No. You can sign in with Apple in one tap if you like, but it's optional, there's no password to make and we don't ask for your email.

How does the app know my level?

When you start, a short 20-question level check covers all six subjects in the same proportions as the exam. It shows your strongest and weakest subject and points your first sessions where you need them. You can skip it and take it later.

Does my progress move between my devices?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs by itself across your iPhone, iPad and Mac, in your own private iCloud. You can also back it up to a file.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Questions, notes and flashcards are all on your device, so you can study on the bus or in a lecture hall with no signal.

How do I get help?

Email chiroboards0@gmail.com or see the Support page.
