Found a mistake in a question or note?

Tap Report a problem under the question or notes section. It opens an email with the question's ID filled in, so we can fix it fast.

Moving to a new phone

If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress appears by itself on a new device a little while after you install ChiroBoards. Without iCloud: open Me, tap Back up progress and save the file to Files. On the new device, open Me, tap Restore and choose that file.

Restore a subscription

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel a subscription

Open Me and tap Manage, or go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
