ChiroBoards Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

ChiroBoards does not collect your personal data. There is no ChiroBoards account or password, no tracking, no ads and no analytics. Signing in with Apple is optional. If you use it, the app keeps an anonymous identifier from Apple on your device so it can recognise you, and may use your first name for the greeting. We do not ask for your email, and none of this is sent to us.

Where your study data is kept

Your answers, progress, flashcards, notes, highlights and exam date are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage, so they stay the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That storage belongs to your Apple Account and is run by Apple. We cannot see or reach it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for ChiroBoards in the Settings app. Deleting the app removes the data from that device. Data in your iCloud stays until you remove it under iCloud storage in the Settings app.

Backups

If you use Back up progress, the app writes your data to a file in a place you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. The file stays under your control. We never receive it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

Your name

If you enter a first name, it is used only for the greeting and the personal watermark on notes. It stays on your device and is not synced.

Children

ChiroBoards is meant for chiropractic students and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use Send feedback in the Me tab, or email chiroboards0@gmail.com.
